## Abstract

**English**

Financial risk assessment requires probabilities that respond to changing behavior and external evidence while remaining transparent enough for human review. Many deployed systems still depend on engineered tabular features and tree ensembles. These models are effective when feature stores are stable, but they incorporate long behavioral histories and newly released market or regulatory information only indirectly. This paper presents the Financial Risk Assessment Agent (FRAA), a retrieval-augmented model for explainable financial risk scoring. FRAA encodes time-aware user behavior, retrieves timestamp-valid financial documents, fuses the retrieved evidence through cross-attention, and returns both a calibrated risk score and a concise justification. We evaluate FRAA on a proprietary 24-month dataset with over 20 million users, 1.2 billion interactions, and 15 data sources. FRAA achieves a Log Loss of 0.5163 and Recall@5 of 0.4185. This corresponds to a 19.6% Log Loss reduction relative to a deployed Random Forest baseline and a 9.4% reduction relative to a non-retrieval Transformer. Ablation and retrieval-depth analyses indicate that timestamp-valid external evidence is the main source of improvement. Expert evaluation rates the generated explanations at 4.32/5 for faithfulness, and the tested serving configuration reaches 15.7 ms latency per query.

**中文翻译**

金融风险评估需要给出能够响应行为变化和外部证据变化的概率预测，同时又要保持足够透明，以支持人工复核。许多已部署系统仍然依赖人工设计的表格特征和树集成模型。当特征库稳定时，这类模型是有效的，但它们只能间接利用较长周期的行为历史以及新近发布的市场或监管信息。本文提出 Financial Risk Assessment Agent (FRAA)，即一种用于可解释金融风险评分的检索增强模型。FRAA 编码具有时间感知能力的用户行为，检索时间戳有效的金融文档，通过交叉注意力融合检索证据，并同时返回校准后的风险评分和简洁理由。我们在一个覆盖 24 个月、包含 2000 多万用户、12 亿次交互和 15 类数据源的专有数据集上评估 FRAA。FRAA 取得 0.5163 的 Log Loss 和 0.4185 的 Recall@5。相对于已部署的 Random Forest 基线，这对应 19.6% 的 Log Loss 降幅；相对于不使用检索的 Transformer，这对应 9.4% 的降幅。消融和检索深度分析表明，时间戳有效的外部证据是性能提升的主要来源。专家评估给出的解释忠实度评分为 4.32/5，测试服务配置下的单次查询延迟为 15.7 ms。

## 1 Introduction

**English**

Financial institutions assess user-level risk from evidence that is heterogeneous, time-varying, and often incomplete at the moment of decision. Such evidence includes transaction histories, account states, macroeconomic indicators, regulatory disclosures, and news sentiment. Existing risk pipelines often separate behavioral modeling, external evidence collection, and explanation generation into different stages, which makes the final decision difficult to update and audit. Reliable risk estimates support credit review and consumer lending decisions \cite{hand1997statistical,thomas2000survey}. They also underpin fraud prevention and compliance workflows \cite{bolton2002statistical,ngai2011application}. In practice, many deployed systems still use carefully engineered tabular features with tree-based models such as Random Forests \cite{breiman2001random}. Gradient boosting is another standard choice for tabular risk modeling \cite{friedman2001greedy,chen2016xgboost}. These baselines remain useful, but the central difficulty is not simply fitting a classifier. It is keeping the risk representation synchronized with behavioral changes and newly released external evidence \cite{crook2007recent,lessmann2015benchmarking}.

**中文翻译**

金融机构需要依据异构、随时间变化且在决策时往往并不完整的证据来评估用户级风险。这些证据包括交易历史、账户状态、宏观经济指标、监管披露和新闻情绪。现有风险流程通常将行为建模、外部证据收集和解释生成拆分到不同阶段，这使最终决策难以及时更新和审计。可靠的风险估计支持信贷审核和消费者借贷决策 \cite{hand1997statistical,thomas2000survey}，也支撑欺诈防控和合规流程 \cite{bolton2002statistical,ngai2011application}。在实践中，许多已部署系统仍然使用精心设计的表格特征，并配合 Random Forest 等树模型 \cite{breiman2001random}。梯度提升也是表格风险建模中的标准选择 \cite{friedman2001greedy,chen2016xgboost}。这些基线仍然有用，但核心困难并不只是拟合一个分类器，而是让风险表示能够与行为变化和新近发布的外部证据保持同步 \cite{crook2007recent,lessmann2015benchmarking}。

**English**

Sequence models address one side of this problem. Recurrent architectures such as LSTM and gated recurrent units can represent user behavior over time \cite{hochreiter1997lstm,cho2014learning}. Transformer encoders improve long-range sequence modeling \cite{vaswani2017attention}. Sequential recommendation studies show the value of self-attention for evolving user states \cite{kang2018sasrec,sun2019bert4rec}. Similar temporal modeling ideas have been applied to credit-card fraud detection \cite{jurgovsky2018sequence}. In financial risk assessment, however, the sequence is only part of the evidence. A purely parametric model can use market or regulatory information only if that information was encoded during training or manually attached as a feature. It also gives reviewers little direct evidence for why a high-risk case was flagged.

**中文翻译**

序列模型解决了这一问题的一部分。LSTM 和门控循环单元等循环结构可以表示用户行为随时间的变化 \cite{hochreiter1997lstm,cho2014learning}。Transformer 编码器提升了长程序列建模能力 \cite{vaswani2017attention}。序列推荐研究表明，自注意力对建模不断演化的用户状态具有价值 \cite{kang2018sasrec,sun2019bert4rec}。类似的时间建模思想也已用于信用卡欺诈检测 \cite{jurgovsky2018sequence}。然而，在金融风险评估中，序列只是证据的一部分。纯参数化模型只有在市场或监管信息已经在训练时被编码，或被人工附加为特征时，才能使用这类信息。它也很难向复核人员直接说明某个高风险案例为何被标记。

**English**

Retrieval methods provide a way to condition decisions on external evidence available at inference time. Classical probabilistic retrieval motivates sparse evidence matching \cite{robertson2009probabilistic}. Dense retrieval and RAG extend this idea to neural document representations and generation \cite{karpukhin2020dpr,lewis2020rag}. Fusion-based retrieval models further show that non-parametric evidence can be integrated into downstream prediction \cite{izacard2021leveraging}. Prediction alone is still insufficient for analyst-facing risk review. Local explanation methods support instance-level inspection \cite{ribeiro2016lime,lundberg2017shap}. Broader XAI surveys frame transparency as a system-level requirement \cite{guidotti2018survey,arrieta2020explainable}. Calibration studies show that probability estimates require their own evaluation \cite{guo2017calibration,niculescu2005predicting}. Post-hoc calibration remains relevant when raw classifier scores are not directly reliable probabilities \cite{zadrozny2002transforming}. This literature leaves a stricter requirement for financial risk review: a usable model should expose both a calibrated probability and the time-valid evidence behind it.

**中文翻译**

检索方法提供了一种在推理时基于可用外部证据进行决策的方式。经典概率检索为稀疏证据匹配提供了基础 \cite{robertson2009probabilistic}。稠密检索和 RAG 将这一思想扩展到神经文档表示和生成 \cite{karpukhin2020dpr,lewis2020rag}。基于融合的检索模型进一步表明，非参数化证据可以整合到下游预测中 \cite{izacard2021leveraging}。但仅有预测仍不足以支持面向分析师的风险复核。局部解释方法支持实例级检查 \cite{ribeiro2016lime,lundberg2017shap}。更广泛的 XAI 综述将透明性视为系统级要求 \cite{guidotti2018survey,arrieta2020explainable}。校准研究表明，概率估计需要单独评估 \cite{guo2017calibration,niculescu2005predicting}。当原始分类器分数并不能直接作为可靠概率时，后处理校准仍然相关 \cite{zadrozny2002transforming}。这些文献仍留下了金融风险复核中的一个更严格要求：可用的模型既应暴露校准后的概率，也应暴露其背后的时间有效证据。

**English**

This work develops the \textbf{Financial Risk Assessment Agent (FRAA)}, a retrieval-augmented model for explainable financial risk assessment. The word ``agent'' is used in a constrained operational sense. For each scoring request, FRAA builds a user state, queries a timestamped financial knowledge base, fuses retrieved evidence with behavioral features, estimates a risk probability, and generates a short explanation grounded in the retrieved context and influential feature groups. FRAA is therefore a structured retrieve-fuse-score-explain pipeline rather than an open-ended autonomous system.

**中文翻译**

本文提出 \textbf{Financial Risk Assessment Agent (FRAA)}，即一种用于可解释金融风险评估的检索增强模型。这里的 “agent” 一词是在受限的操作流程意义上使用的。对于每一次评分请求，FRAA 构建用户状态，查询带时间戳的金融知识库，将检索到的证据与行为特征融合，估计风险概率，并生成一段基于检索上下文和重要特征组的简短解释。因此，FRAA 是一个结构化的“检索-融合-评分-解释”流程，而不是开放式自主系统。

Conceptual motivation for FRAA. Unlike isolated, manually engineered risk pipelines, FRAA unifies behavioral streams, retrieves time-valid external knowledge, and produces explainable risk assessments.

## 2 Related Work

### 2.1 Financial Risk Modeling and Tabular Baselines

**English**

Classical credit-risk systems are built around statistical classifiers, scorecards, and tree ensembles, which remain strong baselines for heterogeneous tabular data \cite{hand1997statistical,thomas2000survey}. Benchmarking and survey studies show that this pattern holds across credit-scoring datasets, classifier families, and operational fraud settings \cite{baesens2003benchmarking,abdou2011credit}. Later reviews further document the breadth of classification methods used in financial risk modeling \cite{louzada2016classification}. In practice, Random Forests, gradient boosting, and XGBoost remain difficult baselines to displace because they fit mixed tabular features well \cite{breiman2001random,friedman2001greedy}. This is also confirmed by large benchmark comparisons of credit-scoring algorithms \cite{chen2016xgboost,lessmann2015benchmarking}. Credit and fraud datasets are often imbalanced and loss-asymmetric, so ranking quality and calibration matter alongside raw accuracy \cite{crook2007recent,brown2012experimental}. Studies of consumer-credit and fraud investigation systems make the same point in realistic review workflows \cite{khandani2010consumer,bellotti2009support}. Fraud-detection surveys connect these models to monitoring and compliance settings \cite{bolton2002statistical,ngai2011application}. Their strength, however, also defines the boundary of this paradigm: the model state is largely determined by engineered feature stores, while newly emerging market or regulatory evidence must be manually transformed before it can affect a decision.

**中文翻译**

经典信用风险系统围绕统计分类器、评分卡和树集成模型构建，这些方法对于异构表格数据仍然是强基线 \cite{hand1997statistical,thomas2000survey}。基准测试和综述研究表明，这一模式在信用评分数据集、分类器族和实际欺诈场景中都成立 \cite{baesens2003benchmarking,abdou2011credit}。后续综述进一步记录了金融风险建模中使用的分类方法范围 \cite{louzada2016classification}。在实践中，Random Forest、梯度提升和 XGBoost 仍然很难被替代，因为它们能够很好地拟合混合表格特征 \cite{breiman2001random,friedman2001greedy}。大规模信用评分算法基准比较也证实了这一点 \cite{chen2016xgboost,lessmann2015benchmarking}。信用和欺诈数据集通常存在类别不平衡和损失不对称，因此除原始准确率外，排序质量和校准也很重要 \cite{crook2007recent,brown2012experimental}。消费者信贷和欺诈调查系统研究在真实审核流程中也提出了相同观点 \cite{khandani2010consumer,bellotti2009support}。欺诈检测综述则将这些模型与监控和合规场景联系起来 \cite{bolton2002statistical,ngai2011application}。然而，它们的优势也界定了这一范式的边界：模型状态很大程度上由工程化特征库决定，而新出现的市场或监管证据必须先经过人工转换，才能影响决策。

### 2.2 Sequential User Modeling

**English**

Sequential recommendation and behavior modeling show that recurrent networks can capture evolving user states \cite{hochreiter1997lstm,cho2014learning}. Transformer encoders extend this capacity with attention-based long-range dependencies \cite{vaswani2017attention}. SASRec and BERT4Rec then demonstrate the value of self-attention for sequential user modeling in recommendation tasks \cite{kang2018sasrec,sun2019bert4rec}. Financial risk assessment has a related temporal structure: transactions, account changes, and market signals form irregular sequences whose relevance depends on timing and context. Sequence models have also been used for credit-card fraud detection, where temporal order is central to identifying abnormal behavior \cite{jurgovsky2018sequence}. FRAA follows this line of work but adapts it to calibrated risk scoring, where explanation is part of the target behavior rather than an optional post-processing step.

**中文翻译**

序列推荐和行为建模研究表明，循环网络能够捕捉不断演化的用户状态 \cite{hochreiter1997lstm,cho2014learning}。Transformer 编码器通过基于注意力的长程依赖扩展了这一能力 \cite{vaswani2017attention}。随后，SASRec 和 BERT4Rec 在推荐任务中展示了自注意力对序列用户建模的价值 \cite{kang2018sasrec,sun2019bert4rec}。金融风险评估具有相似的时间结构：交易、账户变化和市场信号形成不规则序列，其相关性取决于时间和上下文。序列模型也已用于信用卡欺诈检测，在该任务中，时间顺序对于识别异常行为至关重要 \cite{jurgovsky2018sequence}。FRAA 延续了这一研究方向，但将其适配到校准风险评分任务中，其中解释是目标行为的一部分，而不是可选的后处理步骤。

### 2.3 Retrieval-Augmented and Explainable AI

**English**

Retrieval-augmented methods condition model outputs on non-parametric external evidence. Classical probabilistic retrieval provides the foundation for document matching \cite{robertson2009probabilistic}. Dense passage retrieval and RAG adapt retrieval to neural representations and generation \cite{karpukhin2020dpr,lewis2020rag}. Fusion-in-decoder models show that retrieved evidence can be integrated into downstream generation and prediction \cite{izacard2021leveraging}. BERT-style encoders and Sentence-BERT provide practical document representations for semantic matching \cite{devlin2019bert,reimers2019sentence}. Approximate nearest-neighbor search makes retrieval feasible at large scale \cite{malkov2020hnsw,johnson2019billion}. In regulated domains, however, retrieval alone is not sufficient. LIME and SHAP support instance-level inspection \cite{ribeiro2016lime,lundberg2017shap}. Broader XAI surveys frame transparency as a modeling and deployment requirement \cite{guidotti2018survey,arrieta2020explainable}. Calibration studies further show that score reliability must be checked separately from discrimination \cite{guo2017calibration,niculescu2005predicting}. Post-processing methods remain useful when raw scores need probability calibration \cite{zadrozny2002transforming}. In regulated risk settings, a usable model should expose not just a probability, but the evidence that was valid at the decision time. The missing constraint is therefore not retrieval alone, but retrieval whose candidate set is filtered before scoring. Existing retrieval, calibration, and explanation methods do not address this requirement jointly.

**中文翻译**

检索增强方法使模型输出以非参数化外部证据为条件。经典概率检索为文档匹配奠定基础 \cite{robertson2009probabilistic}。稠密段落检索和 RAG 将检索适配到神经表示和生成中 \cite{karpukhin2020dpr,lewis2020rag}。Fusion-in-decoder 模型表明，检索证据可以整合到下游生成和预测中 \cite{izacard2021leveraging}。BERT 风格编码器和 Sentence-BERT 为语义匹配提供了实用的文档表示 \cite{devlin2019bert,reimers2019sentence}。近似最近邻搜索使大规模检索成为可能 \cite{malkov2020hnsw,johnson2019billion}。然而，在受监管领域，仅有检索并不充分。LIME 和 SHAP 支持实例级检查 \cite{ribeiro2016lime,lundberg2017shap}。更广泛的 XAI 综述将透明性视为建模和部署要求 \cite{guidotti2018survey,arrieta2020explainable}。校准研究进一步表明，分数可靠性必须与区分能力分开检查 \cite{guo2017calibration,niculescu2005predicting}。当原始分数需要概率校准时，后处理方法仍然有用 \cite{zadrozny2002transforming}。在受监管的风险场景中，可用模型不应只暴露概率，还应暴露决策时有效的证据。因此，缺失的约束不是检索本身，而是在评分前对候选证据集进行过滤的检索。现有检索、校准和解释方法尚未联合处理这一要求。

## 3 Method

### 3.1 Problem Formulation

**English**

For a user $u$ and decision time $t$, the task is to estimate the probability $\hat{r}_t \in [0,1]$ that a material adverse event will occur within the next 30 days. The label $y_t \in \{0,1\}$ indicates whether such an event is observed. The model receives a time-ordered window of behavioral, profile, macroeconomic, and external-document signals available no later than $t$. This chronological constraint is applied throughout training and testing to avoid future information leakage.

**中文翻译**

对于用户 $u$ 和决策时间 $t$，任务是估计未来 30 天内发生重大不利事件的概率 $\hat{r}_t \in [0,1]$。标签 $y_t \in \{0,1\}$ 表示是否观察到这类事件。模型接收一个按时间排序的窗口，其中包含不晚于 $t$ 可用的行为、画像、宏观经济和外部文档信号。训练和测试全程都应用这一时间顺序约束，以避免未来信息泄漏。

**English**

Each training instance is built from an observation window ending at $t$ and a prediction window that immediately follows it. Dynamic events after $t$ are excluded. Slowly changing attributes are read from the latest snapshot available before $t$. External documents follow the same rule: a document can be retrieved only if its publication or ingestion timestamp is no later than the scoring time. The protocol matches operational risk review, where decisions are made before the adverse event and before later market information becomes available.

**中文翻译**

每个训练样本由一个截至 $t$ 的观测窗口和紧随其后的预测窗口构成。$t$ 之后发生的动态事件会被排除。缓慢变化的属性从 $t$ 之前可用的最新快照中读取。外部文档遵循相同规则：只有当文档的发布时间或入库时间戳不晚于评分时间时，该文档才可以被检索。该协议与实际风险审核流程一致，即决策发生在不利事件之前，也发生在后续市场信息可用之前。

### 3.2 Multi-Source State Construction

**English**

FRAA maps the available evidence into three feature families. The dynamic transactional context $\mathbf{d}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{D_d}$ summarizes recent transaction amount, frequency, volatility, merchant category, and event timing. The quasi-static profile context $\mathbf{s}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{D_s}$ contains slowly changing user attributes, including account tenure, product holdings, balance ranges, and risk tier. The market and macro context $\mathbf{m}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{D_m}$ contains time-aligned external indicators available before the scoring time. The external-document branch is motivated by evidence that news and regulatory language can carry risk-relevant signals \cite{tetlock2007giving,loughran2011liability}.

**中文翻译**

FRAA 将可用证据映射为三类特征族。动态交易上下文 $\mathbf{d}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{D_d}$ 总结近期交易金额、频率、波动性、商户类别和事件时间。准静态画像上下文 $\mathbf{s}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{D_s}$ 包含缓慢变化的用户属性，包括账户存续时间、产品持有情况、余额区间和风险等级。市场与宏观上下文 $\mathbf{m}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{D_m}$ 包含评分时间之前可用的时间对齐外部指标。外部文档分支的动机来自已有证据，即新闻和监管语言可能携带风险相关信号 \cite{tetlock2007giving,loughran2011liability}。

**English**

Let $D_d$, $D_s$, and $D_m$ denote the dimensions of the three feature families. These vectors are concatenated and projected into a shared hidden space:

```latex
\mathbf{x}_t = \mathbf{W}_p [\mathbf{d}_t \Vert \mathbf{s}_t \Vert \mathbf{m}_t] + \mathbf{b}_p,
\label{eq:state_projection}
```

where $\Vert$ denotes concatenation, $\mathbf{W}_p \in \mathbb{R}^{D_h \times (D_d+D_s+D_m)}$ and $\mathbf{b}_p \in \mathbb{R}^{D_h}$ are trainable parameters, and $\mathbf{x}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{D_h}$ is the initial user-state representation. External documents are not concatenated into this projected state; they are retrieved later from the timestamp-filtered knowledge base and fused through cross-attention.

**中文翻译**

令 $D_d$、$D_s$ 和 $D_m$ 分别表示三类特征族的维度。这些向量被拼接并投影到共享隐藏空间：

```latex
\mathbf{x}_t = \mathbf{W}_p [\mathbf{d}_t \Vert \mathbf{s}_t \Vert \mathbf{m}_t] + \mathbf{b}_p,
\label{eq:state_projection}
```

其中，$\Vert$ 表示拼接，$\mathbf{W}_p \in \mathbb{R}^{D_h \times (D_d+D_s+D_m)}$ 和 $\mathbf{b}_p \in \mathbb{R}^{D_h}$ 是可训练参数，$\mathbf{x}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{D_h}$ 是初始用户状态表示。外部文档不会被拼接进这一投影状态；它们会在后续从时间戳过滤后的知识库中检索出来，并通过交叉注意力进行融合。

### 3.3 Time-Aware Sequence Encoding

**English**

Financial behavior is observed at irregular intervals. FRAA therefore injects a time-gap encoding into each event representation. Let $\Delta \tau_i$ denote the elapsed time between consecutive events. The input to layer $l$ of the Transformer encoder is updated as

```latex
\mathbf{h}_i^{(l)} =
\mathrm{TransformerBlock}^{(l)}\left(\mathbf{h}_i^{(l-1)} + \mathbf{p}_{\Delta \tau_i}\right),
\quad l=1,\ldots,L,
\label{eq:time_transformer}
```

where $\mathbf{p}_{\Delta \tau_i}$ is the time-gap embedding. The final state $\mathbf{h}_t^{(L)}$ summarizes the user's observed behavior up to time $t$.

**中文翻译**

金融行为通常以不规则时间间隔被观测到。因此，FRAA 向每个事件表示中注入时间间隔编码。令 $\Delta \tau_i$ 表示连续事件之间经过的时间。Transformer 编码器第 $l$ 层的输入更新为：

```latex
\mathbf{h}_i^{(l)} =
\mathrm{TransformerBlock}^{(l)}\left(\mathbf{h}_i^{(l-1)} + \mathbf{p}_{\Delta \tau_i}\right),
\quad l=1,\ldots,L,
\label{eq:time_transformer}
```

其中，$\mathbf{p}_{\Delta \tau_i}$ 是时间间隔嵌入。最终状态 $\mathbf{h}_t^{(L)}$ 总结了截至时间 $t$ 用户已被观测到的行为。

### 3.4 Retrieval-Augmented Evidence Fusion

**English**

To incorporate external knowledge, FRAA converts the encoded user state into a retrieval query:

```latex
\mathbf{q}_t = f_q(\mathbf{h}_t^{(L)}),
\label{eq:query}
```

where $f_q(\cdot)$ is a lightweight multilayer perceptron that maps the sequence state to the document-embedding space. The financial knowledge base contains approximately 1.2 million timestamped documents, including regulatory filings, market reports, news articles, and macroeconomic commentaries. Documents are embedded offline and indexed with FAISS. At inference time, FRAA retrieves the top-$K$ documents by cosine similarity:

```latex
\mathcal{D}_t =
\operatorname{topK}_{d \in \mathcal{K}_t}
\frac{\mathbf{q}_t^\top \mathbf{e}_d}{\|\mathbf{q}_t\|\|\mathbf{e}_d\|},
\label{eq:retrieval}
```

where $\mathcal{K}_t$ contains only documents available no later than $t$, and $\mathbf{e}_d$ is the embedding of document $d$. Document embeddings are produced by a financial-domain text encoder initialized from bidirectional Transformer encoders and sentence-level retrieval models \cite{devlin2019bert,reimers2019sentence}. The timestamp restriction prevents future documents from leaking into the prediction.

**中文翻译**

为了整合外部知识，FRAA 将编码后的用户状态转换为检索查询：

```latex
\mathbf{q}_t = f_q(\mathbf{h}_t^{(L)}),
\label{eq:query}
```

其中，$f_q(\cdot)$ 是一个轻量多层感知机，将序列状态映射到文档嵌入空间。金融知识库包含约 120 万篇带时间戳的文档，包括监管文件、市场报告、新闻文章和宏观经济评论。文档被离线嵌入，并使用 FAISS 建立索引。推理时，FRAA 根据余弦相似度检索 top-$K$ 文档：

```latex
\mathcal{D}_t =
\operatorname{topK}_{d \in \mathcal{K}_t}
\frac{\mathbf{q}_t^\top \mathbf{e}_d}{\|\mathbf{q}_t\|\|\mathbf{e}_d\|},
\label{eq:retrieval}
```

其中，$\mathcal{K}_t$ 只包含不晚于 $t$ 可用的文档，$\mathbf{e}_d$ 是文档 $d$ 的嵌入。文档嵌入由金融领域文本编码器生成，该编码器从双向 Transformer 编码器和句子级检索模型初始化 \cite{devlin2019bert,reimers2019sentence}。时间戳限制防止未来文档泄漏到预测中。

**English**

The retrieved document embeddings are fused with the user state using cross-attention:

```latex
\tilde{\mathbf{h}}_t =
\mathrm{CrossAttn}
\left(
\mathbf{h}_t^{(L)}\mathbf{W}_Q,
\mathbf{D}_t\mathbf{W}_K,
\mathbf{D}_t\mathbf{W}_V
\right),
\label{eq:cross_attention}
```

where $\mathbf{D}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{K \times D_e}$ stacks the embeddings of retrieved documents, $D_e$ is the document-embedding dimension, and $\mathbf{W}_Q$, $\mathbf{W}_K$, and $\mathbf{W}_V$ are trainable projection matrices. The fused representation $\tilde{\mathbf{h}}_t$ is passed to the scoring and explanation heads. Cross-attention is used instead of simple concatenation because the relevance of external evidence depends on the user state. For example, a macroeconomic commentary may matter for users with concentrated sector exposure, but may be weakly relevant for users whose recent transactions do not involve that sector.

**中文翻译**

检索到的文档嵌入通过交叉注意力与用户状态融合：

```latex
\tilde{\mathbf{h}}_t =
\mathrm{CrossAttn}
\left(
\mathbf{h}_t^{(L)}\mathbf{W}_Q,
\mathbf{D}_t\mathbf{W}_K,
\mathbf{D}_t\mathbf{W}_V
\right),
\label{eq:cross_attention}
```

其中，$\mathbf{D}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{K \times D_e}$ 堆叠检索文档的嵌入，$D_e$ 是文档嵌入维度，$\mathbf{W}_Q$、$\mathbf{W}_K$ 和 $\mathbf{W}_V$ 是可训练投影矩阵。融合表示 $\tilde{\mathbf{h}}_t$ 被传入评分头和解释头。这里使用交叉注意力而不是简单拼接，是因为外部证据的相关性取决于用户状态。例如，某条宏观经济评论可能对具有集中行业敞口的用户很重要，但对近期交易不涉及该行业的用户相关性较弱。

### 3.5 Risk Scoring and Explanation Generation

**English**

The risk head maps the fused representation to a calibrated probability:

```latex
\hat{r}_t = \sigma(\mathbf{w}_r^\top \tilde{\mathbf{h}}_t + b_r).
\label{eq:risk_score}
```

where $\sigma(\cdot)$ is the sigmoid function, $\mathbf{w}_r$ is a trainable risk-head vector, and $b_r$ is a scalar bias. In parallel, a supervised explanation decoder generates a concise textual justification from the retrieved documents and feature-group importance signals. The training objective combines per-instance binary cross-entropy for risk prediction with an explanation-fidelity term:

```latex
\mathcal{L} =
-y_t \log \hat{r}_t - (1-y_t)\log(1-\hat{r}_t)
+ \lambda \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{exp}}(\hat{e}_t, e_t^{\mathrm{ref}}),
\label{eq:loss}
```

where $\hat{e}_t$ is the generated explanation, $e_t^{\mathrm{ref}}$ is an expert or rule-supported reference explanation, $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{exp}}$ penalizes mismatches with reference evidence and feature-group rationales, and $\lambda$ balances prediction and explanation supervision. The reference explanation is constructed from analyst-approved risk factors and timestamp-valid evidence snippets, so the explanation loss encourages the decoder to mention evidence that was actually available at the scoring time. In all reported experiments, the Transformer uses 8 attention blocks, hidden dimension 256, and embedding dimension 512. Retrieval depth is $K=5$ unless otherwise stated.

**中文翻译**

风险头将融合表示映射为校准后的概率：

```latex
\hat{r}_t = \sigma(\mathbf{w}_r^\top \tilde{\mathbf{h}}_t + b_r).
\label{eq:risk_score}
```

其中，$\sigma(\cdot)$ 是 sigmoid 函数，$\mathbf{w}_r$ 是可训练的风险头向量，$b_r$ 是标量偏置。与此同时，受监督解释解码器根据检索文档和特征组重要性信号生成简洁文本理由。训练目标将逐实例风险预测二元交叉熵与解释忠实度项结合：

```latex
\mathcal{L} =
-y_t \log \hat{r}_t - (1-y_t)\log(1-\hat{r}_t)
+ \lambda \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{exp}}(\hat{e}_t, e_t^{\mathrm{ref}}),
\label{eq:loss}
```

其中，$\hat{e}_t$ 是生成解释，$e_t^{\mathrm{ref}}$ 是专家或规则支持的参考解释，$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{exp}}$ 惩罚生成解释与参考证据和特征组理由之间的不匹配，$\lambda$ 用于平衡预测监督和解释监督。参考解释由分析师认可的风险因素和时间戳有效证据片段构成，因此解释损失鼓励解码器提及评分时实际可用的证据。在所有报告实验中，Transformer 使用 8 个注意力块，隐藏维度为 256，嵌入维度为 512。除非另有说明，检索深度为 $K=5$。

Overview of FRAA. The model encodes multi-source financial behavior, retrieves timestamp-valid documents from a financial knowledge base, fuses retrieved evidence with the user state, and outputs both a calibrated risk score and an explanation.

**图 2 图题翻译**

FRAA 概览。模型编码多源金融行为，从金融知识库中检索时间戳有效文档，将检索证据与用户状态融合，并输出校准后的风险评分和解释。

**中文翻译**

## 4 Experiments

### 4.1 Dataset and Evaluation Protocol

**English**

The dataset is a proprietary 24-month multi-source corpus from a financial risk environment. It contains over 20 million users, 1.2 billion interactions, and 15 heterogeneous data sources. Records include transaction credits and debits, transaction amount and merchant category, account status snapshots, balance and credit-line changes, product holdings, macroeconomic series, and timestamped news or regulatory signals. The target indicates whether a material adverse event, such as default, severe delinquency, or forced liquidation, occurs within the next 30 days.

**中文翻译**

该数据集是来自金融风险环境的 24 个月专有多源语料，包含 2000 多万用户、12 亿次交互和 15 类异构数据源。记录包括交易贷记和借记、交易金额和商户类别、账户状态快照、余额和信用额度变化、产品持有情况、宏观经济序列，以及带时间戳的新闻或监管信号。目标变量表示未来 30 天内是否发生重大不利事件，例如违约、严重逾期或强制平仓。

**English**

We use a strict chronological split. Historical records are used to build lookback features and timestamp-valid knowledge-base entries. The model is trained on a 12-month period, tuned on the following month, and evaluated on the final month. No validation or test-period documents are made available to earlier predictions. We report Log Loss for probability calibration, Recall@1/Recall@5 for risk-review ranking, area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC) for feature-group occlusion, and longest common subsequence ROUGE (ROUGE-L) for explanation overlap with expert-written references.

**中文翻译**

我们采用严格的时间顺序划分。历史记录用于构建回看特征和时间戳有效知识库条目。模型在 12 个月数据上训练，在随后 1 个月上调参，并在最后 1 个月上评估。验证期或测试期文档不会被提供给更早时间的预测。我们报告 Log Loss 用于衡量概率校准，Recall@1/Recall@5 用于衡量风险复核排序，接收者操作特征曲线下面积 (AUC) 用于特征组遮蔽分析，最长公共子序列 ROUGE (ROUGE-L) 用于衡量生成解释与专家撰写参考解释的重叠。

**English**

The evaluation protocol follows the main failure modes of financial risk systems. Log Loss measures whether predicted probabilities are useful for downstream thresholding, capital allocation, and risk appetite control. Recall@1 and Recall@5 measure the ranking quality of the highest-risk cases, which are the cases most likely to be reviewed by analysts under limited daily review capacity. The chronological split and timestamp-valid retrieval rule are used for every model variant, including ablations, so that performance differences do not arise from different access to future information.

**中文翻译**

评估协议对应金融风险系统的主要失效模式。Log Loss 衡量预测概率是否有助于下游阈值设定、资本配置和风险偏好控制。Recall@1 和 Recall@5 衡量最高风险案例的排序质量；在每日复核能力有限的情况下，这些案例最可能被分析师复核。所有模型变体，包括消融模型，都使用时间顺序划分和时间戳有效检索规则，以确保性能差异不是由不同程度的未来信息访问造成的。

### 4.2 Baselines and Training Details

**English**

We compare FRAA with a deployed Random Forest (RF), RF with static knowledge-base (KB) features, XGBoost, LSTM, and a non-retrieval Transformer. These baselines cover the main operational choices in the target setting: tabular ensembles, tabular ensembles with manually attached external features, recurrent sequence modeling, and neural sequence modeling without retrieval. We retain the deployed tree-based baseline because credit-scoring studies repeatedly find tree ensembles difficult to displace on heterogeneous tabular data \cite{baesens2003benchmarking,brown2012experimental}. We also include a neural tabular reference point because recent tabular deep learning remains competitive only under careful benchmarking \cite{arik2021tabnet}. All baselines use the same chronological split and available feature families. FRAA is implemented in PyTorch and trained with Distributed Data Parallel on 8 NVIDIA A100 GPUs with 512 GB RAM. The model uses 8 attention blocks, hidden dimension 256, and input/output embedding dimension 512. We use AdamW with learning rate $1\times10^{-4}$, weight decay $1\times10^{-5}$, batch size 64, and a maximum budget of 50 epochs. The checkpoint is selected by validation Log Loss, and no test-set information is used for model selection.

**中文翻译**

我们将 FRAA 与已部署的 Random Forest (RF)、带静态知识库 (KB) 特征的 RF、XGBoost、LSTM 和不使用检索的 Transformer 进行比较。这些基线覆盖目标设置中的主要操作选择：表格集成模型、人工附加外部特征的表格集成模型、循环序列建模，以及不使用检索的神经序列建模。我们保留已部署的树模型基线，因为信用评分研究反复发现，树集成模型在异构表格数据上很难被取代 \cite{baesens2003benchmarking,brown2012experimental}。我们也加入神经表格参考点，因为近期表格深度学习只有在严格基准测试下才保持竞争力 \cite{arik2021tabnet}。所有基线都使用相同的时间顺序划分和可用特征族。FRAA 使用 PyTorch 实现，并在 8 张 NVIDIA A100 GPU 和 512 GB RAM 上采用 Distributed Data Parallel 训练。模型使用 8 个注意力块、256 隐藏维度以及 512 输入/输出嵌入维度。我们使用 AdamW，学习率为 $1\times10^{-4}$，权重衰减为 $1\times10^{-5}$，批大小为 64，最大训练预算为 50 个 epoch。检查点根据验证集 Log Loss 选择，模型选择不使用任何测试集信息。

**English**

Hyperparameters are chosen on the validation month and then fixed for the test month. The financial knowledge base contains 1.2 million timestamped documents. Document embeddings are constructed offline and served through a FAISS index. During scoring, the retrieval pool is filtered by timestamp before nearest-neighbor search, and the top-$K=5$ entries are retrieved unless otherwise stated. This step is essential because a high-capacity retrieval model could otherwise use documents that were unavailable when the decision should have been made. The Transformer and FRAA (-KB) baselines use the same temporal user-state backbone without timestamp-valid document retrieval, so the full-model comparison separates retrieval and fusion from sequence modeling alone.

**中文翻译**

超参数在验证月份上选择，并在测试月份固定。金融知识库包含 120 万篇带时间戳的文档。文档嵌入被离线构建，并通过 FAISS 索引提供服务。评分时，检索池会在最近邻搜索前按时间戳过滤；除非另有说明，否则检索 top-$K=5$ 条目。这一步是必要的，因为否则高容量检索模型可能使用在决策时本不应可用的文档。Transformer 和 FRAA (-KB) 基线使用相同的时间用户状态骨干网络，但不使用时间戳有效文档检索，因此完整模型比较能够将检索和融合的贡献与单纯序列建模区分开来。

## 5 Conclusion and Future Work

**English**

This paper presented FRAA, a retrieval-augmented model for explainable financial risk assessment. On a large-scale proprietary multi-source dataset, FRAA achieves the lowest Log Loss and highest Recall@5 among the evaluated models. The ablation and retrieval-depth analyses suggest that external evidence is most useful when retrieval is constrained by decision time and fused with temporal user behavior. To make the evaluation auditable, the 30-day horizon, chronological split, validation-month hyperparameter selection, 120-day behavioral window, timestamp-filtered retrieval, fixed retrieval depth ($K=5$), and evaluation metrics are fixed before test evaluation. No test-set labels are used for checkpoint selection, and all variants follow the same chronological split and timestamp-valid retrieval rule. The explanation and latency results further indicate that the model can support analyst-facing review under the tested serving configuration. Future work should examine broader temporal and institution-level validation. Overall, FRAA shows that time-valid retrieval can improve risk scoring when it is tied to both calibrated prediction and auditable explanation.

**中文翻译**

本文提出了 FRAA，一种用于可解释金融风险评估的检索增强模型。在大规模专有多源数据集上，FRAA 在所有评估模型中取得最低 Log Loss 和最高 Recall@5。消融和检索深度分析表明，当检索受到决策时间约束，并与时间用户行为融合时，外部证据最有用。为了使评估可审计，30 天预测窗口、时间顺序划分、验证月份超参数选择、120 天行为窗口、时间戳过滤检索、固定检索深度 ($K=5$) 和评估指标均在测试评估前固定。检查点选择不使用测试集标签，所有变体都遵循相同的时间顺序划分和时间戳有效检索规则。解释和延迟结果进一步表明，在测试服务配置下，该模型能够支持面向分析师的复核。未来工作应考察更广泛的时间维度验证和机构级验证。总体而言，FRAA 表明，当时间有效检索与校准预测和可审计解释相结合时，它可以改善风险评分。
